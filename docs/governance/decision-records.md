# Decision Records

Decision records capture durable project and architecture decisions together with their rationale and consequences.

## Recommended structure

Each decision record should contain:

- context;
- decision;
- alternatives considered;
- consequences;
- evidence or constraints;
- status.

## Status

Use explicit states such as `PROPOSED`, `ACCEPTED`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `REJECTED`.

A decision record explains why a choice was made. It does not substitute for a specification or implementation evidence.
